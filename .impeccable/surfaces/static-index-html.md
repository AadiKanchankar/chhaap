Scope: the whole Chhaap local app (static/index.html + app.css + app.js), plus hidden /study. Mode: Operate.
Audience/job (v2, TASK2.md): a designer writes a brief, picks a product, edits the plan, prints a design on a slow CPU, sees it on a product mockup, checks which symbols survived, reads the meaning card, exports print files. Examiners inspect the stages, including the planner and the survival check.
Constraints: brief-pinned palette (geru, rice paste, charcoal, one accent), one display + one text face, mono only in under-the-hood, no gradients/glass/emoji, 1280px and phone width.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The app is a block-printer's sample book on the workbench: each stage is a proof card pinned to a geru-plastered board, carrying registration marks and a job-ticket number. It refuses the SaaS sidebar-plus-KPI-card dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Geru red-ochre board, rice-paste proof sheets, charcoal ink; one accent, block-print indigo, only for the live action and active state. Square corners, inked 2px rules, corner registration crosses, swatch-book tabs, stamp buttons that press down. Rozha One display, Hind text, JetBrains Mono inside the under-the-hood sheet only.
STORY: The visitor writes or picks a brief, chooses a drawn product card, adjusts the plan card (layout, palette swatches, story beats as symbol chips), presses Print design, watches the denoising proofs fill a strip, sees the design on its mockup with a caption, checks what survived under the hood, reads what each present (and lost) symbol means, exports print files with the label attached.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead strip with wordmark and machine chip. Left third: The brief sheet (brief box, example chips, product cards, Read the brief), plan card below. Right two thirds: the proof book, Design tab first: the mockup plate with Print design as the single indigo stamp under it.
FORM: textile sample/pattern book (list position 3), seed key 000d043d. Raise from the split-flap board: numbers sit in fixed tabular cells. Raise from the zoo map: flat, unmodulated colour fields, no texture tricks.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
